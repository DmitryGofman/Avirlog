## Description
```
AvirLog is a calm, private journal for breath awareness, inspired by the
Swara Yoga tradition — the practice of noticing which nostril is dominant
right now.

In Swara Yoga, the breath is said to flow more through the left nostril (Ida),
the right nostril (Pingala), or both together (Sushumna) at different times of
day. AvirLog lets you simply observe and record that, then look back at what
your day looked like.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• One-tap logging — Left, Right, or Both, from a clean home screen
• Optional mood, energy and focus notes with each entry
• Insights — see how your breath states move across the day
• A Learn guide explaining the Swara Yoga tradition in plain language
• Gentle reminders with Left / Right / Both quick-log buttons right in the
  notification (long-press the reminder to reveal them)

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account required
• Everything stays on your device
• No analytics, no tracking, no ads
• Export your data anytime

AvirLog is an educational and journaling tool based on the Swara Yoga
tradition and general wellness practice. It is not medical advice and is not
intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.
```
