EVIDENCE · WHAT'S KNOWN VS. BELIEVED

Claims, graded honestly

Every statement in this app carries its evidence level. We don't confirm beliefs — we label them.
Established

The nasal cycle is real

Airflow dominance alternates between nostrils in an ultradian rhythm (~30 min–4 h), driven by autonomic control of nasal blood vessels. Documented since 1895 and confirmed with rhinomanometry.

Kayser 1895 · Eccles 1996 · White et al. 2015
Emerging

Link to autonomic state

Small studies associate nostril dominance with sympathetic/parasympathetic balance, and unilateral forced breathing with modest autonomic shifts. Suggestive — small samples, replication needed.

Werntz et al. 1983 · Telles et al. 1994 · Price & Eccles 2016
Preliminary

Effects on mood & cognition

Claims that dominance predicts focus, energy or mood are not established. This is exactly what your own log can explore — as personal data, n of 1.

Mixed findings · low-powered studies
Traditional

The Swara interpretation

Swara Yoga read this rhythm millennia before instruments could measure it — left as cooling (Ida), right as heating (Pingala), balance as stillness (Sushumna). A remarkable early observation; its meanings remain a hypothesis, honored here as heritage.

Shiva Svarodaya (trad.)
